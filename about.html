About Us

Discover Authentic Chiang Rai with Local Experts

Our story

Your local travel partner since 2016

Welcome to Bamboo Tours Chiang Rai, your local travel partner since 2016. We are passionate about sharing the natural beauty, rich culture, and hidden treasures of Northern Thailand with travellers from around the world.

We offer a variety of experiences, including trekking tours, sightseeing tours, private car and driver services, and authentic Thai cooking classes. Explore lush forests and beautiful waterfalls, discover Chiang Rai’s famous temples, experience local village life, or enjoy a comfortable private journey at your own pace.

Our knowledgeable local guides are passionate about sharing the region’s nature, history, traditions, and local way of life. We aim to make every journey personal, enjoyable, and memorable.

Whether you seek adventure, cultural experiences, or a relaxing holiday, let Bamboo Tours help you discover the real Chiang Rai beyond the ordinary tourist routes.

Explore Nature, Experience Culture.Your unforgettable journey starts with Bamboo Tours Chiang Rai!
