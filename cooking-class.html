Lanna Home Cooking Class

Cook · Learn · Eat · Enjoy — experience the authentic flavours of Northern Thailand, with your own hands.

Half day · morning or afternoon

Cook a full Thai meal, then sit down and enjoy it

฿1,650 per person · minimum 2 participants

Start at a local market shopping for fresh ingredients, walk through the organic garden where much of the produce is grown, then cook four or five dishes of your own choice — soup or curry, a stir-fry, a salad and a dessert.

Price includes

  • Drinking water
  • Visit a local market
  • Cooking ingredients & equipment
  • Recipe booklet to take home
  • Round-trip hotel transfers within Chiang Rai city

All dietary requirements are welcome. Please tell us about any food allergies or dietary requirements in advance.
